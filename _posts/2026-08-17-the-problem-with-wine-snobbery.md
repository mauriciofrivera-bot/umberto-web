One of the biggest problems in the wine world is not what 's in the glass.

It’s the people.

Or more specifically:
the constant need some people have to use knowledge as way to feel superior.

You know those moments when someone uses overly complicated terminology just to impress the table?

Or corrects somebody’s French pronunciation?

Or laughs because someone ordered a “commercial” wine?

That’s exactly why many people feel disconnected from this world.

### Wine should create pleasure.

### Not ego competitions.

Over the years, I’ve seen guests almost embarrassed to order what they genuinely enjoyed.

And honestly, that’s ridiculous.

### Nobody should feel judged over a glass of wine.

Sometimes the simplest choice is exactly the right one for that moment.

And truthfully, many people constantly trying to sound like experts are also the ones enjoying the experience the least.

Because they’re too busy trying to prove something.

### Wine should not be an intelligence test.

It should be connection, curiosity, emotion.

### A great sommelier doesn’t make people feel small.

They make people feel comfortable.

And honestly, I think the future of wine depends on that too:


### less elitism, more humanity.
