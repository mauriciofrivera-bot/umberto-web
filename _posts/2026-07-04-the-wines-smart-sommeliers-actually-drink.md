###### There’s a funny idea in the wine world:

that sommeliers constantly drink rare and extremely expensive bottles.

Reality is often completely different.

Many sommeliers, when drinking for genuine pleasure, look for honest wines.

# Not necessarily famous.
Not necessarily expensive**.**

Wines with energy.
Identity.
Sense of place.

Because when you work with wine every day, eventually you stop chasing labels.

You start chasing emotion.

And often, you find it in the least predictable places.

A volcanic red served slightly chilled.
A Falanghina by the sea.
An old Riesling opened almost accidentally.
A simple bottle shared after an exhausting service.

Many wines that impress people online are not the wines professionals actually drink in everyday life.

Because drinking wine and performing wine are two completely different things.

# And honestly, maybe wine becomes most beautiful exactly when you stop trying to impress anyone.
